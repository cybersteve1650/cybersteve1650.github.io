The starting point

Japan taxes high income heavily

The top national income tax rate is 45%. Resident tax adds a flat 10% on top. At the highest band you keep only 45 yen of every extra 100 yen you earn.

So the fastest way to build wealth here is not to earn more. It is to lower the income that gets taxed.

Lever 03 · Dependent deductions

Support family, lower your tax

If you send money to family who depend on you, in Japan or overseas, you can claim a deduction for each person.

¥380,000 General dependent, age 16 to 18 or 23 to 69
You keep ¥190,000
¥480,000 Elderly dependent, age 70 and above
You keep ¥240,000

The deduction is claimed per person, so several family members can qualify.

Four rules that decide if your claim is accepted

  1. Prove the relationshipA document such as a birth certificate, translated officially into Japanese.
  2. Send the full amount inside one monthSend to all dependents in the same month so everything sits on one statement.
  3. Keep bank statement evidence from your side onlyStatements must show the money leaving your own account.
  4. Submit at the year end tax adjustmentGive the evidence to your employer during the YETA.

Lever 04 · NISA

Tax free investing for life

Normally dividends and capital gains are taxed at 20.315%, made up of 15% income tax, 5% residence tax and 0.315% special reconstruction tax. Inside a NISA account you pay none of it.

¥3.6MEach year, split into ¥1.2M stable and ¥2.4M growth
¥18MLifetime contribution limit

Open an account with Rakuten Securities, SBI or Monex. I use Rakuten Securities. A diversified portfolio of stocks and ETFs works well. You can withdraw at any time, but check the reinvestment rules before you do.

Lever 05 · Furusato nozei

Turn tax you already owe into goods you want

You donate to local governments around Japan and receive gifts in return, such as wagyu, rice and furniture. Almost the whole donation comes back off your resident tax.

  1. Work out your limitFirst reduce your income by your other deductions, such as property and dependents. Then use the calculator.
  2. Donate before 31 DecemberChoose the towns and pick your gifts.
  3. Receive the goodsThey arrive through the year, direct from the town.
  4. Pay less resident taxIn June the following year you see the reduction on the small white and green paper, equal to your donation up to your allowance.
¥564,000 Roughly what you can donate on a ¥20,000,000 salary

The gifts are capped at 30% of the donation value, so about ¥169,200 of free goods each year.

Lever 06 · Permanent residence

The step that unlocks everything else

Permanent residence is not a tax lever by itself. You can buy property without it, but it improves your financing and removes a lot of risk elsewhere.

100% property loans

Banks lend the full purchase price, so you need very little cash up front.

A wider range of banks

More lenders will consider you, which means better rates and terms.

Security if you lose your job

You can remain in Japan. Your status no longer depends on your employer.
